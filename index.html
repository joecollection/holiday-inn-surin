Structural Demand Drivers
1
Surin commands Phuket's highest ADR sub-market
+21% ADR growth in 2025 driven by luxury resort demand and severely constrained beachfront land supply. Holiday Inn's current ฿3,000 ADR trails the market by ~47%.
2
Phuket airport exceeding capacity — Phase II adds 44%
17.4M passengers in 2025 exceeded design capacity. Phase II expansion by 2030 unlocks structurally higher long-haul arrivals, directly benefiting west-coast upper-scale resorts.
3
Diversified source markets reducing seasonal risk
Russia (2024 top market), India, China recovery, UK/Australia/Israel growth. Thailand's neutral geopolitical positioning and expanded visa exemptions drive year-round demand.
4
Phuket pivoting to "Luxury First" — rate over volume
ADR grew 5% in 2025 despite slight occupancy decline, signalling disciplined pricing by operators. ADR leverage strategy is now standard practice for upper-scale Phuket hoteliers.
5
IHG franchise — live loyalty & distribution from Day 1
IHG Rewards (100M+ members), GDS, OTA brand boost, corporate contracts and digital booking infrastructure already in place following 2023 rebrand.
