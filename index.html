Why This Opportunity Matters

Phuket remains one of Asia’s strongest resort markets with continued tourism recovery, resilient international demand, and increasing premium travel spending. Karon Beach benefits from high international visibility while maintaining lower replacement barriers versus ultra-luxury beachfront zones.


Comparable branded resort assets in Phuket continue experiencing ADR growth driven by:


  • Long-stay international tourism growth
  • Family travel demand normalization
  • Wellness tourism expansion
  • Experiential hospitality trends
  • Strong airlift recovery into Phuket International Airport
  • Increasing institutional appetite for Thai hospitality assets
